How it works

  1. 1

    Enter measurements

    Vital signs and lab values from a routine check-up. Optional fields can be left blank — the model imputes them from typical values.

  2. 2

    Model estimates risk

    A tuned neural network or classical model, selected by 5-fold cross-validation among seven algorithms, outputs a probability.

  3. 3

    Understand the result

    See the risk band, the measurements that raised or lowered it, and save it to the assessment history.
