About MediPredict

Diabetes, cardiovascular disease and chronic kidney disease are among the leading causes of death and disability in India and worldwide, and all three are often detected late. MediPredict is a decision-support prototype that turns routine clinical measurements into an early, explainable risk estimate.

Approach

  • Data cleaning: physiologically impossible zeros treated as missing; median / most-frequent imputation; standardisation; one-hot encoding of nominal variables.
  • Models compared: Logistic Regression, K-Nearest Neighbours, Support Vector Machine, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and an Artificial Neural Network (Multilayer Perceptron).
  • Validation: stratified 80/20 split, 5-fold cross-validated grid search, model selection on CV ROC-AUC only, decision threshold tuned on out-of-fold predictions (Youden's J).
  • Explainability: global permutation importance and per-patient "what drove this result" analysis.

Limitations

  • The datasets are small and come from specific populations (Pima women aged 21+, Cleveland Clinic, one Indian hospital); results may not generalise.
  • The CKD dataset is almost perfectly separable, so its near-perfect scores should be read with caution.
  • The tool is for education and screening support only — it is not a medical device and not a diagnosis.
